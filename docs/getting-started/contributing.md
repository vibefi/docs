---
title: Contributing
---

Developers can get involved in the VibeFi DAO by reading open issues across the [VibeFi repositories](https://github.com/vibefi) and picking one to work on.

If you want a bigger challenge, check the monorepo issue tracker:

- [vibefi/monorepo issues](https://github.com/vibefi/monorepo/issues)

Those issues often cover larger efforts that span entirely new products, codebases, or business lines.
